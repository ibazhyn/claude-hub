# Learnings — durable repository facts

> What Sessions learned the hard way, so the next Session does not pay for it again: commands that really
> work (and from which directory), environment quirks, flaky tests, hazards, traps that destroyed work.
> Workers propose entries in their result files (`LEARNINGS CANDIDATES`); the orchestrator merges them here
> after QA. Session MDs quote the hazards that apply to their repository.
>
> Record properties and how to check them, not remembered values: "the suite resolves its runner from the
> local `node_modules`", not "runner 4.0.18". A hazard entry states what happens, how to avoid it, and the
> date and Session it was learned in. When an entry turns out wrong, correct it in place.
